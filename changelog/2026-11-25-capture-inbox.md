DataDeck opens with your most recent web captures. Paste or drop a URL and start typing or dictating in the notes area while the app finds the title, images, charts and downloadable tables. Revisit a note from the log, or capture the same URL again to move it to the top. [Watch the capture walkthrough](https://github.com/datasets/datadeck/blob/main/docs/videos/library.mp4).
